Scientific Methodology & Uncertainty Notice

Thermal indices are calculated using the Steadman Heat Index equation and empirical WBGT micro-modeling calibrated explicitly for tropical maritime-urban microclimates. Environmental readings represent atmospheric conditions at standardized measurement heights and do not substitute for direct physiological core temperature measurements. Individual susceptibility varies based on personal acclimatization, occupational hydration schedule, and personal protective clothing.
